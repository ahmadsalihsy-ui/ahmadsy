# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
A simple interest calculator that calculates the interest based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Inputs
- Principal: The initial amount of money.
- Rate: The annual interest rate in percentage.
- Time: The time period in years.

## Example
- Principal: 1000
- Rate: 5%
- Time: 2 years

Calculation:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

Total Amount = 1000 + 100 = 1100

## Features
- Calculates simple interest.
- Calculates the total amount.
- Easy to understand and use.
